## Report content or a user

1. Open the post, comment, or profile.
2. Tap the safety / menu control (⋯ or flag icon).
3. Choose Report and pick a reason (Inappropriate, Harassment, or Spam).

Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.

## Block a user

1. Open their profile, or use the safety menu on their post/comment.
2. Choose Block and confirm.

You can unblock someone later from their profile.

## Delete your account

1. Open your Profile tab.
2. Tap Account Settings.
3. Scroll to Delete account and confirm.

Deletion permanently removes your profile and associated account data. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email roshpaul117@gmail.com from the address on your account and request deletion.
