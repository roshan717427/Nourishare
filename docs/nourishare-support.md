## Report content or a user

**On a profile**

1. Open the user’s profile.
2. Tap **Report/Block** (top right).
3. Choose **Report**.
4. Pick a reason: Inappropriate, Harassment, Spam, or Other. For Other, type a short explanation.

**On a post or comment**

1. Open the post or comment.
2. Tap the **⋯** (safety) menu.
3. Choose **Report**, then pick a reason as above.

Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.

## Block a user

1. Open their profile and tap **Report/Block**, or use the **⋯** menu on their post/comment.
2. Choose **Block** and confirm.

You can unblock someone later from their profile (**Report/Unblock** → Unblock).

## Delete your account

1. Open your Profile tab.
2. Tap Account Settings.
3. Scroll to Delete account and confirm.

Deletion permanently removes your profile and associated account data. This cannot be undone. Reports involving your account and block-list references to your username are cleaned up on a best-effort basis.

If you cannot access the app, email roshpaul117@gmail.com from the address on your account and request deletion.
