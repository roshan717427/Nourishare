Nourishare Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026
Contact: roshpaul117@gmail.com

Nourishare (“we”, “us”) is a cooking and social recipe app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using Nourishare, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address, password (stored by Firebase Authentication), username, display name, and optional profile photo.
  • Profile and cooking data: kitchen preferences, personality traits, meal logs, ratings, photos of meals, meal plans, pantry ingredients you enter, and AI suggestion history tied to your account.
  • Social data: follows and follow requests, likes, comments, reports you submit, and users you block.
  • Device data: push notification tokens (if you enable notifications) so we can send follow and interaction alerts.
  • Technical data: standard server logs needed to operate and secure our API (for example timestamps and error diagnostics). We do not sell personal information.

2. How we use information

  • Provide sign-in, profiles, feeds, meal logging, and meal planning.
  • Generate AI recipe suggestions using cooking preferences and pantry inputs you provide. Prompts are processed by our servers and Google Gemini; do not enter sensitive personal data in free-text fields.
  • Enforce community safety (reports, blocks, content filters, account bans for Terms violations).
  • Send optional push notifications related to social activity.

3. Where data is stored

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and host our API on Vercel. Meal and profile photos you upload may be stored as image data associated with your Firestore documents. Service providers process data only to provide their services to us.

4. Sharing

Content you post (recipes, photos, comments) is visible to users you allow through the app’s follow model. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users from serious harm or abuse.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Account Settings → Delete account). That removes your Firebase Auth account and deletes or cleans up associated profile data, logs, social edges, meal plans, and AI usage data on a best-effort basis. Some residual references (for example copies of comments in others’ threads) may take additional cleanup time.

6. Children

Nourishare is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices

  • Update profile fields in the app.
  • Block or report other users.
  • Disable notifications in system settings.
  • Delete your account as described above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new effective date at the top of this page. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact
