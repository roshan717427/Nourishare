## Privacy Policy

Nourishare (“we”, “us”) is a cooking and social recipe app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using Nourishare, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

- Account data: email address, password (stored by Firebase Authentication), username, display name, and optional profile photo.

- Profile and cooking data: kitchen preferences, personality traits, meal logs, ratings, photos of meals, meal plans, pantry ingredients you enter, and AI suggestion history tied to your account.

- Social data: follows and follow requests, likes, comments, reports you submit, and users you block.

- Device data: push notification tokens (if you enable notifications) so we can send follow and interaction alerts. Delivery uses Expo’s push service.

- Technical data: standard server logs needed to operate and secure our API (for example timestamps and error diagnostics). We do not sell personal information.

2. How we use information

- Provide sign-in, profiles, feeds, meal logging, and meal planning.

- Uploaded photos may be checked for safety (Gemini) before posting.

- Generate AI recipe suggestions using your cooking preferences, meal history, pantry inputs you provide, and friends’ public cooking activity (for example meal titles and usernames visible through the app’s follow model). Prompts are processed by our servers and Google Gemini; do not enter sensitive personal data in free-text fields.

- Enforce community safety (reports, blocks, content filters, account actions for Terms violations).

- Send optional push notifications related to social activity (via Expo Push).

3. Where data is stored

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and host our API on Vercel. Meal and profile photos you upload may be stored as image data associated with your Firestore documents. Push delivery is handled by Expo. Service providers process data only to provide their services to us.

4. Sharing

Content you post (recipes, photos, comments) is visible to users you allow through the app’s follow model. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users from serious harm or abuse.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Account Settings → Delete account). That removes your Firebase Auth account and deletes or cleans up associated profile data, posts, likes, social connections, meal plans, and AI usage data on a best-effort basis. Comments you left on other users’ posts may remain in those threads with the author shown as “Deleted User.” Reports involving your account (as reporter or reported user) and references to your username on other users’ block lists are removed on a best-effort basis when your account is deleted.

6. Children

Nourishare is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices

- Update profile fields in the app.

- Block or report other users.

- Disable notifications in system settings.

- Delete your account as described above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will update the effective date at the top of this document. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
